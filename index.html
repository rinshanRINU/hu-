About HU (Happy User)

Bridging consumer delight with premier investor credibility and high-tier retail logistics.

HU (Happy User) is a UAE-based consumer brand selling electronics, mobile accessories and smart gadgets to everyday customers through retail, distribution, export and online channels, including our storefront myhustore.com.
